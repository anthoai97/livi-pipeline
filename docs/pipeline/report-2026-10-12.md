# Pipeline report, 2026-10-12: room rules (#54)

What issue [#54](https://github.com/anthoai97/livi-pipeline/issues/54) changed,
measured against `main` after #55. How the pipeline works:
[overview](pipeline-overview.md) and [details](pipeline-details.md). Plan:
[54-room-rules](../plans/54-room-rules.md).

## Summary

- **Better:** rooms come out more complete (reviewer completeness 3.29 -> 3.66),
  dining rooms gain a pendant and a sideboard, the tight dining room recovers
  (overall 2.22 -> 3.22, spend 44% -> 82%), and 38 of 39 runs give three
  different layouts. Speed and cost are unchanged.
- **Worse:** the 3 x 3 m bedroom loses its bed in 3 of 9 designs (0 of 9
  before), and the base living room's layout scores drop by 0.5 to 1.0 because
  two required accent chairs crowd the coffee table.
- **Not ready to merge** until the small bedroom no longer ships designs
  without a bed. Decisions needed are at the end.

## Setup

- **Requests:** all 13 cases in `pipeline/scripts/benchmark_requests.json`, 3
  runs each: 39 runs, 117 designs, on two services.
- **Before:** `main` after #55 (the #55 benchmark, 2026-10-11). **After:**
  branch `issue-54-room-rules`.
- **Model:** `gemini-3.8-flash` at low thinking; Jev rank and check on; local
  catalog. Reviewer `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium effort, scores 1 to 5, all 117
  designs on both sides.
- The two compact rooms (`bedroom_small`, `studio_small`) were rerun after the
  compact-foot fix below; the tables use the first run for all 13 cases and
  the rerun is reported separately.

## Problems found

1. **The 3 x 3 m bedroom ships designs without a bed.** First run: 5 of 9
   designs. The 0.91 m foot rule on a door route made a 2.1 m bed impossible
   in a 3 m room; the fix (`6ba9585`, compact rooms keep 0.60 m) cut it to 3
   of 9 in a rerun. The rest: the model picks a 1.66-1.82 m wide bed with two
   nightstands and a sideboard, the solver cannot fit them for four turns, and
   the final check, which removes non-anchor items first but may remove the
   anchor last, removes the bed. The deeper door zone (0.8 m for this door)
   adds to it. The small studio, rerun with the same fix, delivers every bed
   and sofa.
2. **The base living room is crowded.** Two accent seats are now required in a
   spacious living room; the reviewer measures them 0.10-0.36 m from the coffee
   table and scores circulation 3 on every design. It also calls the 0.44 m
   sofa-to-table gap "tight", which is within the new 0.38-0.50 m rule, so part
   of the drop is the reviewer disagreeing with the rule.
3. **Required pieces push large rooms over budget.** The large bedroom has 13
   over-budget errors (3 before) and 14 "missing clothes storage" errors: the
   model drops the dresser to fit the budget, then fails for not having one.
   It ends complete (8 of 9 with storage) but takes 15 s longer. The large
   dining room shows the same pattern with the pendant (9 over-budget errors,
   2 before).
4. **The Scandi living room fails more turns** (12 -> 21), mostly on the new
   coffee table height rule (0.35-0.50 m) and the existing brief-wide attribute
   check. Its scores are unchanged.

## Decisions needed

1. **Never remove the room's anchor in the final check** (bed, sofa, dining
   table): deliver the design with its blocking finding instead. Recommended;
   a bedroom without a bed is worse than a crowded one. Or keep the current
   order and tighten what the model may pick for a compact bedroom.
2. **Living room accent seats:** require one in medium and spacious rooms, not
   two in spacious ones, or keep two and give them more room in the solver.
3. **Budget order for required pieces:** let budget repair swap a required
   piece for a cheaper one of the same role, instead of the model dropping it.

## Not done

- Solver costs for a rug 0.3 m wider than the sofa and a 0.65 m seat pitch.
- Art height: 1.50 m is the bottom of the frame, not its center (as before).
- No `painting` slot: the catalog has no paintings.
