# Pipeline report, 2026-10-12: room rules (#54)

What issue [#54](https://github.com/anthoai97/livi-pipeline/issues/54) changed,
measured against `main` after #55. How the pipeline works:
[overview](pipeline-overview.md) and [details](pipeline-details.md). Plan:
[54-room-rules](../plans/54-room-rules.md).

## Summary

- **Better:** the best overall reviewer score of the three rounds (3.13 ->
  3.25), rooms more complete (3.29 -> 3.74), dining rooms with a pendant and a
  sideboard, the tight dining room recovered (overall 2.22 -> 3.11), and fewer
  designs over their room's furniture limit (16 -> 11 of 117). No design loses
  its bed, sofa or dining table.
- **Worse:** runs are slower (median 33.1 -> 40.5 s; all still under 60 s) and
  cost a little more ($4.70 -> $5.15 for 39 runs). The base living room scores
  below `main` (overall 3.67 -> 3.33), within chance at 9 designs.
- **Ready for a PR.**

## Setup

- **Requests:** all 13 cases in `pipeline/scripts/benchmark_requests.json`, 3
  runs each: 39 runs, 117 designs per round, on two services.
- **Rounds:**
  - **main:** `main` after #55 (2026-10-11).
  - **#54 v1:** parts 1-3 (thresholds and bugs, completeness, texts).
  - **#54 final:** v1 plus the compact bed-foot fix and the crowding fix.
- **Model:** `gemini-3.8-flash` at low thinking; Jev rank and check on; local

  designs in every round.

### Floor furniture limit

Share of usable floor (doors excluded) that floor furniture may cover,
comfortable target / maximum. Rugs, wall and ceiling pieces, tabletop items and
a TV on its stand do not count.

| Room | Compact | Medium | Spacious |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Living room | 22% / 30% | 27% / 35% | 30% / 38% |
| Bedroom | 42% / 50% | 35% / 43% | 30% / 38% |
| Dining room | 32% / 40% | 30% / 38% | 28% / 36% |
| Studio | 35% / 43% | 32% / 40% | 30% / 38% |

The prompt asks for the target. Over the maximum is a selection error, or a
warning when the required pieces alone exceed it or after a fit step, so a
small room never loops on it.

## Known issues

1. **Slower runs.** Median 33.1 -> 40.5 s, first design 21.1 -> 27.2 s, from
   more selection turns on the required pieces and the size checks. Every run
   still finishes under 60 s.
2. **The small living room stays over its limit** because its requested pieces
   alone exceed 30% of 9 m2. Raise the compact living maximum, or accept the
   warning.
3. **The base living room is below main** on layout (overall 3.67 -> 3.33),
   within chance; the reviewer still calls the 0.44 m sofa-to-table gap tight,
   which is inside the 0.38-0.50 m rule.
4. **The early fit estimate** ("room too small" warning) still uses its old
   size-only ratios, so a room can pass it and then hit the new limit at
   selection.
