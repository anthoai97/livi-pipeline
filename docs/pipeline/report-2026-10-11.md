# Pipeline report, 2026-10-11

Where the pipeline stands after the work of October 10-11, measured on `main`
with the default model. How the pipeline works: [overview](pipeline-overview.md)
and [details](pipeline-details.md).

## Setup

- **Requests:** the six cases in `pipeline/scripts/benchmark_requests.json`
  (living room, Scandi living room, bedroom, dining room, studio, relaxed
  studio), 3 runs each: 18 runs, 54 designs.
- **Model:** `gemini-3.8-flash` at low thinking for every stage; Jev rank and
  check on; local catalog.
- **Commands** (from `pipeline/`): `python scripts/benchmark.py --runs 3`, then
  `python scripts/review.py --runs <ids>` (reviewer `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium
  effort, scores 1 to 5).
- **Baseline:** the end of phase 5 (2026-10-10, same cases and setup).

## What did not work

- **Room profiles** (one file per room type) grew into a large general
  framework before a new room type was known. Step 1 is parked on the branch
  `issue-22-room-profiles`. New rooms follow the
  [room-type guide](adding-a-room-type.md).
- **Living room decor.** Living rooms add no decor beyond the required plant,
  even with budget left. The selection model treats the plant as the decor.
  Studios do pick decor.
- **Prompt-only room use.** Asking for more furniture without fixing the side
  table candidates raised neither piece counts nor scores.

## Known issues

1. **Brief-wide attributes block the Scandi living room.** Jev checks wording
   like "boucle textures", "low wooden furniture" and "soft neutrals" against
   every piece, so lamps and tables fail it.
   - This caused 38 selection failures across the 9 Scandi designs, 2.78 turns
     on average, and removals on the last turn.
   - It is also why Scandi spends a median of only 72%.
2. **No fill after last-turn removals.** A selection that loses pieces on its
   last turn is not filled back to 80%. Most of the 7 designs under 80% are
   Scandi.
3. **Studios score lowest** on overall layout and use of space (3.0).
4. **Brief reading can spike** to 37 s or more. That latency is on Gemini's
   side.

## Next

1. Apply brief-wide attributes only to pieces they can describe, or make them
   advice instead of a rejection.
2. Run the budget fill after last-turn removals.
3. Look at the reviewer's studio complaints before changing studio rules.
